BreatheSafe — AQI & Body Impact Tracker
Live ↗
Personal air-quality dashboard

A location-aware air pollution tracker that turns a raw AQI number into something the person can actually act on. It auto-detects the user's location via GPS, pulls real-time PM2.5, PM10, and NO₂ readings for that exact spot, and shows a live AQI score with a clear severity band (Good → Hazardous) instead of just a number nobody can interpret.

what makes it different

  • Converts ambient PM2.5 into an estimated personal dosage based on how long the user has been exposed, rather than just displaying the outdoor reading.
  • Runs that dosage through an anatomical risk model that scores impact separately for lungs, heart, brain, and eyes — so "412 AQI" becomes "high risk to lungs and heart specifically."
  • Live outdoor-exposure timer that tracks cumulative PM2.5 / PM10 / NO₂ intake for a session in real time, not just a static daily average.
  • Mask-effectiveness engine that compares N95, surgical, and cloth masks at the current AQI, showing the actual filtered percentage and effective AQI behind each option.
  • Recovery protocols tab with concrete, condition-specific steps to reduce and reverse the impact once exposure has happened, not just a warning to "stay indoors."
